The problem

India manages diabetes by looking in the rear-view mirror

101 M
Indians living with diabetes, plus 136 M with prediabetes (ICMR-INDIAB, 2023)
1 in 3
or fewer reach glycaemic targets. Most complications are driven by uncontrolled excursions
~26,000
CGM + smartwatch readings per patient between two 3-monthly OPD visits, that no doctor can read
  • HbA1c is an average. It hides the post-meal spikes that carbohydrate-heavy Indian diets (rice, wheat) cause every day.
  • Care is reactive. The doctor sees what already happened, never what is about to happen.
  • Advice is generic. "Eat less rice" ignores that two patients respond to the same meal in completely different ways.

The challenge: fuse the static record (EHR) with the dynamic stream (wearables) into a model that predicts adverse events before they happen and that a doctor can actually use.

Our solution

A virtual patient the doctor can query, not just a risk score

Early warning

"Spike likely in 90 min"

Forecasts glucose at 30 / 60 / 120 min with calibrated uncertainty, and flags excursions above 180 mg/dL early enough to act: a walk or a smaller portion.

What-if

"What if she eats jowar roti instead?"

The doctor simulates meals, portions and post-meal walks on this patient's own calibrated physiology and sees the curve before prescribing.

Explained

"Why is the risk high?"

Every alert lists its patient-specific drivers (meal, sleep debt, HRV, trend, physiology) and a pre-consultation summary is drafted automatically.

Who uses it: endocrinologists and GPs in OPD and tele-consults, diabetes educators for diet counselling. Next: patients and ASHA workers via vernacular alerts.

Why this is a digital twin

Five properties that separate a twin from a predictor

1

Personalised

Six physiological parameters fitted to each patient, starting from a prior built from their EHR.

2

Synchronised

A state observer pulls the twin toward every new CGM reading. The twin is always "now".

3

Predictive (hybrid)

ML learns what physiology leaves out (sleep debt, stress, dawn effect) from the wearable + EHR streams.

4

Simulatable

Mechanistic core answers counterfactuals that a pure ML model cannot.

5

Actionable

Triage, explanations and summaries in the doctor's workflow.

Static EHR stream × dynamic wearable stream → a physiological replica, updated every 5 minutes.

Static: demographics, diagnoses, HbA1c history, labs, medications, TCF7L2 genotype, polygenic risk (HL7 FHIR R4)

Dynamic: CGM, heart rate, HRV, steps, sleep stages, meal log (Apple Health / Google Fit-style)

Twin core

A calibrated physiological model, kept in sync with the patient

G′ = −(SG + X + A)·G + SG·Gb + Ra(t)/V
X′ = p2·(kx·max(G − Gb, 0) − X)
A′ = (k_ex·cadence − A)/15

  • EHR prior: Gb ← fasting glucose, insulin action ← HbA1c and disease status
  • MAP calibration of Gb, SG, kx, p2, gut speed, exercise sensitivity on days 1–10
  • Observer corrects state at every CGM reading
  • Meal absorption speed derived from carbs, GI, fat, protein, fibre

The twin recovers the hidden physiology of each synthetic patient: Spearman ρ = {{rho_kx}} (insulin action), {{rho_gb}} (basal glucose). Personal calibration cuts the twin's forecast loss by {{calib_gain}}% vs the EHR-only prior.

Hybrid ML layer

Machine learning corrects what physiology cannot see

  • ~85 fused features per 5-min step: CGM trends, steps, HR, HRV vs personal baseline, last night's sleep, logged carbs and glycaemic load, EHR, twin-simulated trajectories, carbs on board, insulin action
  • LightGBM forecasts Δglucose at 30 / 60 / 120 min
  • Conformalised quantile regression gives 90% intervals with guaranteed coverage
  • Event classifiers for spikes (>180) and hypos (<70) within 2 h

Alert fatigue is a safety issue

The alert threshold is chosen as the most sensitive operating point with ≤ 1 false alert per patient-day on held-out patients, not by maximising a statistic. Alerts must persist 10 min and are snoozed for 60 min, like CGM alarms.

Explainable by design

TreeSHAP contributions are grouped by data stream and translated into plain language: "carbs eaten in the last hour", "sleep debt", "HRV below personal baseline".

Leakage-proof

Every feature uses only data available at time t. A unit test perturbs the future and checks that present features do not change.

Honest limitations & roadmap

What a proof of concept can and cannot claim

Limitations

  • Synthetic results are an upper bound: we designed that physiology
  • CGMacros is small (n = {{real_n}}), US-based and has no sleep / HRV
  • Meal GI not annotated in real data (default used)
  • Decision support only; not a medical device

Roadmap

  • Validate on Indian CGM cohorts with partner diabetologists
  • Twin upgrades: particle-filter state estimation, medication-titration simulation
  • Patient app: vernacular alerts, WhatsApp / SMS for ASHA workers
  • ABDM sandbox integration and a prospective clinic pilot
